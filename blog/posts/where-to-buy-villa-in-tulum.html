Where to Buy a Villa in Tulum: Aldea Zama and Beyond

Published May 15, 2026 · 10 min read

Tulum has several areas to consider for villa acquisition, each with distinct characteristics. For buyers, the choice of location affects demand, operational ease, and resale value. Aldea Zama stands out as a strong option, but understanding other zones is also helpful.

Aldea Zama: A Planned Community for Buyers

Aldea Zama is one of the Tulum areas buyers often compare because it offers a more planned residential setting and access to services.

Advantages of Aldea Zama:

  • Security: Aventuras Villas properties include 24/7 security, which can matter to both owners and guests.
  • Infrastructure: Buyers should still verify roads, utilities, internet, and building services before purchasing. Villa Sorella includes Starlink internet up to 200 Mbps. Buyers should confirm internet details property by property.
  • Amenities: Proximity to restaurants, coworking spaces, shops, and other conveniences.
  • Proximity to Beach: While not beachfront, Aldea Zama is a reasonable distance from Tulum's beaches, typically 5-10 minutes by car or 15 minutes by bike.

Other Areas in Tulum to Consider for Buyers

Beyond Aldea Zama, Tulum offers other zones, each with different considerations for a buyer's profile:

Beach Zone

  • Buyer Consideration: Potential for direct beach access, boutique hotel proximity, and a vibrant dining scene.
  • Points to Verify: Higher prices, stricter building regulations, limited development opportunities, and potential for more noise/traffic.

La Veleta

  • Buyer Consideration: A developing area that some buyers look at for a more bohemian feel, potentially offering newer construction.
  • Points to Verify: Infrastructure can be less developed in some parts, and consistency of services may vary.

Region 15

  • Buyer Consideration: Another developing area that some buyers assess for a mix of residential and commercial projects, sometimes with a focus on eco-friendly designs.
  • Points to Verify: Similar to La Veleta, infrastructure is still evolving, and some areas may feel more isolated.

How to Compare Areas Effectively

When evaluating where to buy, consider these practical points:

  • Long-Term Vision: How do municipal development plans align with your investment horizon?
  • Actual Use: Is the property primarily for personal use, rental, or a mix? This influences desired amenities and location.
  • Operating Costs: Factor in property taxes, HOA fees, utility costs, and potential property management fees.
  • Infrastructure Reliability: Verify the consistency of water, electricity, and internet services in your chosen area.
  • Local Expertise: Partner with a knowledgeable real estate agent and legal counsel who understand Tulum's specific market dynamics and regulations.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a fideicomiso?
A fideicomiso is a bank trust that allows foreign individuals to legally own property in Mexico's restricted zones (coastal areas and borders). The bank holds the legal title, and the foreign buyer is the beneficiary with full ownership rights.

Is Aldea Zama good for rental use?
Aldea Zama can make sense for rental use because guests often value security, access to restaurants and services, and reasonable distance to the beach. Buyers should still verify demand, operating costs, and management assumptions before relying on rental income.

How far are the villas from the beach?

Can foreigners legally buy villas in Tulum?
Yes, foreigners can legally buy villas in Tulum, typically through a fideicomiso (bank trust) when purchasing in coastal or border areas.

What is the main advantage of buying in Aldea Zama?
The main advantage is the combination of a more planned residential setting, access to services, and reasonable distance to the beach. Buyers should still verify the property, the building, and the operating assumptions before purchasing.
