Direct Booking vs OTA: What Guests Should Know

Published March 11, 2026 · 5 min read

Both direct and OTA booking can be valid. The best option depends on your priorities.

Direct Booking Advantages

  • Potentially better rates or bonuses
  • Faster direct communication with host team
  • More flexibility on custom requests

OTA Advantages

  • Familiar checkout flow
  • Loyalty perks (if applicable)
  • One dashboard for multiple stays

Checklist Before Confirming

  • Cancellation terms
  • Total fees and taxes
  • Check-in instructions
  • Support contact response time

For Aventuras Villas, if you want concierge coordination before arrival, direct communication usually helps most.
