5-Day Tulum Itinerary for Villa Guests

Published March 11, 2026 · 6 min read

This itinerary is built for guests who want a beautiful trip without feeling rushed. Think one anchor activity per day, one optional add-on, and enough margin to actually enjoy where you are.

Day 1 — Arrive and settle in

  • Transfer + grocery stop
  • Pool reset + simple dinner
  • Early rest for a better Day 2

Day 2 — Beach and town flow

  • Early beach window
  • Lunch and local walk
  • Relaxed evening plan

Day 3 — Cenote focus

  • Leave early
  • Primary cenote + optional second stop
  • Light evening back at villa

Day 4 — Adventure or wellness

  • Choose one main experience
  • Keep the day balanced, not packed

Day 5 — Slow departure rhythm

  • Brunch and final photos
  • Smooth checkout and transfer timing
