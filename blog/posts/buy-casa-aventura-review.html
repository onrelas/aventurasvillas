Questions to Ask Before Booking Any Villa in Tulum

Before finalizing your villa rental, consider these important questions to ensure a smooth and enjoyable trip:

  • Is the beach walkable? Confirm the exact walking distance and accessibility if beachfront access is a priority.
  • Is transportation required? Understand if a rental car, taxi, or bicycle is necessary for getting around from your villa's location.
  • What services are included? Clarify what amenities and services (e.g., cleaning, utilities, pool maintenance) are part of your rental package.
  • Is cleaning included? Determine the frequency of cleaning services and if extra costs apply for additional cleaning.
  • What is the cancellation policy? Review the terms and conditions for cancellations or changes to your reservation.
  • What internet service is available? Verify the type, speed, and reliability of the internet connection, especially if you plan to work remotely.
