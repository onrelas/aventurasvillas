Tulum Events Calendar (Guest Planning Edition)

Updated March 12, 2026

This calendar is designed to help guests choose better travel windows and avoid avoidable surprises. Event timing can change, so use this as a planning map and verify close to your dates.

Annual planning calendar

  • January–March: high demand season, strong weather consistency, earlier booking recommended.
  • April–June: transition period, often better value with good flexibility.
  • July–September: warmer and rain-prone periods, plan with backup options.
  • October–November: shoulder period that can balance pricing and crowd level.
  • December: holiday demand rises quickly; book villas early.

Monthly event-check rhythm (recommended)

  1. 30–45 days before arrival: check local event density and traffic risk.
  2. 14 days before arrival: confirm activity bookings and transport timing.
  3. 3 days before arrival: final weather/event adjustment and day-by-day tweaks.
