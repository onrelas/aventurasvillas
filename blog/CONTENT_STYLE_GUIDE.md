# Aventuras Villas Blog Voice & Publishing Guide

## Voice
- Warm, welcoming, and premium (never generic).
- Practical + emotionally reassuring.
- Write like a local host who truly cares about guest experience.
- Avoid robotic listicle tone.

## Structure for each post
1. Human opening that feels personal and useful.
2. Clear, actionable sections with simple language.
3. "Local host" notes that reduce guest stress.
4. Booking CTA block at the end.

## Conversion rules
- Include one direct booking CTA and one discovery CTA.
- Use "less stress", "better flow", "more time to enjoy" framing.
- Keep paragraphs short for mobile readers.

## Publishing cadence
- 2 posts per week.
- Focus topics: planning, logistics, neighborhood tips, booking confidence, local experiences.
